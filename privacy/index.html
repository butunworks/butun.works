Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

bütün Works is an independent studio in Istanbul that builds apps and agentic AI systems. This policy covers our websites (butun.works and vivivi.store) and the tools we run ourselves. A product that handles more personal data will publish its own policy, and for that product its own policy applies.

Our websites

Our websites are static pages. They set no cookies, run no analytics or advertising, and have no forms that collect your data.

  • The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to run and protect the service (GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you open a page (Google Privacy Policy).

Data from Google APIs

Some of our own tools sign in to Google with our own accounts, for example to keep encrypted backups of our servers in our Google Drive. These tools ask only for the access they need (for Drive, only the files the tool itself created) and use that access only for this purpose. We do not sell this data, share it with anyone, or use it to train AI models.

bütün Works' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties, except where the law requires it.
